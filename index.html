1890

Herman Hollerith built the machine that counted America. We tabulate the web.

The 1880 census had taken about eight years to tally by hand. Hollerith’s punch cards turned each person into a record a machine could count, and the 1890 count of 63 million Americans went dramatically faster. We’re named after him.
